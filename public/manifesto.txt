01. THE PROBLEM: ENCAPSULATION
Digital content has been caged. Corporate DRM (Digital Rights Management)
isn't just about copyright; it’s about control. They own the player, the
stream, and the data. The signal is filtered, compressed, and monitored.

02. THE SOLUTION: THE MESH
GLST Systems exists to provide a "Web-Based Intercept." We don't just stream
music; we transmit raw data.

The Shield: Our DRM-Shield logic (AudioContext Interception) ensures that
the listener isn't just a consumer, but a node in the network.

The Scramble: A manual protocol to break tracking algorithms and
signal-profiling bots.

The Bounty: Direct-to-Source uplink. No middle-man, no corporate delay. A
financial signal sent directly into the mesh.

03. THE ARCHITECTURE
We prioritize function over comfort.

Monospace Only: Human readability is secondary to data integrity.

High Contrast: Cyan on Black. The colors of the night-shift operator.

Scanlines: A visual reminder that every digital image is just a series of
lines being drawn in real-time.
